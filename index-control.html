Engine Management Systems (EMS) manage various aspects of a vehicle’s engine, including fuel injection, ignition timing, and other critical engine functions.

• 16 general purpose analog inputs
• 1 PT200 input
• 1 PT1000 input
• 6 digital inputs
• 8 low side outputs
• 8 low side outputs with current feedback
• 3 low side high current outputs
• 2 full H-bridge outputs
• 2 CAN lines optoisolated
