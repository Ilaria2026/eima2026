AgriSIC

Agri SIC is a complete in‑vehicle system designed for off‑highway applications, based on a centralized computing architecture capable of managing and controlling multiple displays while ensuring synchronized data and content sharing across the system.
The high-performance central unit enables the use of compact display solutions, optimizing cockpit layout and reducing system complexity.
