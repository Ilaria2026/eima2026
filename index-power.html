BHP22 On-Board Charger

BHP22 is a 22 kW bidirectional, high-power-density power module designed for on-board battery-powered vehicle applications.

It combines versatility, safety and high efficiency, leveraging the latest SiC semiconductor and power conversion technologies to deliver outstanding performance even in harsh operating conditions.
The BHP22 can be connected to either single-phase or three-phase AC power and operates in both Forward Charger Mode and Reverse Power Mode, enabling bidirectional functionality and V2L applications.
The BHP22 has been successfully tested in compliance with international EMC, safety and environmental standards.


• 22 kW AC three-phase bidirectional operation (7.3 kW AC single phase)
• Parallelable in charge and export power mode
• AC charger - SAE J1772 and EN 61851 compliance
• DC fast charging (CCS) communication – ISO 15118 and DIN 70121 compliance
• Export power (V2L) and V2G (ISO 15118 compliance)
• AC plug locking/unlocking management
• HVDC interlock monitoring
• Typical efficiency >94%
• AC/DC galvanic isolation
• Functional safety (ASIL-B) and cybersecurity compliant
• IP67, IP6K9K protection rating
• HVDC outputs are short-circuit and reverse polarity protected
• Fully CAN monitored and controlled (v2.0B and FD)
• SAE J1939 and ISO 14229 (UDS) compliant
• UL1741, IEEE1547 compliant

Traction Inverter

Inverters represent the core of the electric propulsion system, converting the battery's direct current into alternating current to power high-performance motors and actuators.
The traction inverter is designed for medium-sized commercial vehicles but can be adapted to a wide range of applications requiring power levels up to 150 kW.
Its wide operating voltage range, from 48 V to 1000 V, ensures maximum flexibility and versatility.

• 3-phase motor controller / inverter
• Voltage range 48-900 VDC
• DC current measure
• Max electrical output power 190 kW
• Multiple resolver / encoder compatibility
• Four quadrant operation
• Variable switching frequency
• High power and current densities
• Adaptable to different types of three-phase motors
• 11 internal / external temperature sensors
• Liquid cooling, 10-15 L/min
• Out spare, 4 Low Side + 2 Hi Side; configurable for alarms or other use
• Connectors: PowerLok Amphenol G2 (1 x 3P + 1 x 2P), 1 x RTS014N19P03 19P Circular connector for Motor Signals, 1 x ATV04-38PC-S2 38P rectangular connector for signals
• Configurable bleed resistor
• High voltage leakage measurement (optional)
• IP65
• Gore valve

High Voltage Battery Management System

The Battery Management System (BMS) monitors and manages lithium-ion batteries, optimizing performance while minimizing aging effects.
The BMS is designed to oversee and control high-voltage battery systems in hybrid and electric vehicles, as well as in motorcycles and stationary applications.

• 6 HV voltage sense
• 6 temperature reading (of which 4 on busbars)
• 6 contactor drivers (precharge, discharge, recharge)
• 4 CAN communication to vehicle powertrain
• Isolated communication interface to CSC Stack
• Support for external current monitor
• 2 HVIL active management
• Pyroswitch driver
• HV insulation monitoring
• ISO 26262 – Up to Asil Level C
• CAN communication management
